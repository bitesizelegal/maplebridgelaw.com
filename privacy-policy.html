Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Maple Bridge Law LLC (“Maple Bridge Law,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website (maplebridgelaw.com) and through our communications with you, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It also governs text (SMS) messages sent by Maple Bridge Law LLC and by our affiliated title agency, Jenkins Title, LLC.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Contact form. When you use the form on our Contact page, we receive the information you enter, which may include your name, email address, phone number, subject, and message.
  • Communications. When you call, email, or text us, or engage us for legal or title services, we receive the information you share with us in the course of that relationship, including transaction and matter information.
  • SMS consent. If you check the SMS consent box on our form or otherwise give us express permission, we record your phone number and the fact, date, and manner of your consent.

Information collected automatically

This website is a static site. We do not run advertising trackers or analytics scripts of our own. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and operational purposes. Some pages embed third-party content, such as Google Maps and Google Fonts, which may set cookies or collect usage data under Google’s own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and requests.
  • To provide legal, title, and closing services and to communicate with you about your matter or transaction.
  • To send transactional text messages you have opted in to receive, such as closing confirmations, document requests, appointment reminders, and transaction updates.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and professional obligations, including record-keeping and conflict-checking requirements.
  • To protect against fraud and unauthorized activity, including wire fraud.

3. Text (SMS) Messaging

We send text messages only to people who have opted in, either by checking the SMS consent box on our website or by giving us express consent in another way. Our messaging program is transactional. We use it for closing confirmations and reminders, document requests, appointment confirmations, and updates about your matter or transaction. We do not use it to send marketing or promotional messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, and you can get help by replying HELP or contacting us using the information below.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be used for any purpose other than the service for which you have opted in.

The service providers that deliver our text messages on our behalf (for example, our case-management and messaging platform and the mobile carriers) process your phone number only as needed to deliver the messages you have requested.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers. With vendors that help us operate our practice and this website, such as website hosting, email, case-management and messaging platforms, electronic-signature services, and document storage. These providers may use your information only to perform services for us.
  • Your transaction. With parties involved in your matter or closing, such as lenders, title insurance underwriters, other parties to the transaction and their counsel, surveyors, and government recording offices, as needed to perform the services you have engaged us for and consistent with our professional obligations.
  • Legal requirements. When required by law, court order, subpoena, or the rules of professional conduct, or when necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.
  • Affiliates. Between Maple Bridge Law LLC and Jenkins Title, LLC as needed to provide legal and title services on the same transaction. Mobile opt-in information is never shared with affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain client and transaction records for the periods required by law and by the rules of professional conduct that govern lawyers and title agents. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information, such as Social Security numbers or bank account details, through the website contact form.

6. Your Choices

  • Text messages. Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You may also contact us by phone or email to be removed from text messaging.
  • Email. You may ask us at any time to stop sending non-essential email.
  • Access and correction. You may ask us to review or correct the personal information we hold about you. We will honor those requests to the extent consistent with our legal and professional record-keeping obligations.

7. Children

This website is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them through the site.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to other sites, including jenkinstitle.com and social-media platforms. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised policy on this page with a new effective date.
